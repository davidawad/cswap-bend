## claude-swap 0.27.2

cswap-bend is a Bend 2 port of [claude-swap](https://github.com/realiti4/claude-swap).
`parity/contract.md` records its observable behaviour, the `parity/fixtures`
were produced by running it, and `reference/patches` are diffs against its source
(`reference/fetch` downloads the original and does not store it here).
